The **Broadcom BCM2711** is the system‑on‑chip (SoC) used in the Raspberry Pi 4 and some industrial/embedded
Raspberry‑Pi‑compatible boards. It is a major upgrade over earlier Raspberry Pi SoCs and brings proper 64‑bit performance to
the family. [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raspberry_Pi_4)

### CPU and architecture

- **CPU cores:** Quad‑core ARM Cortex‑A72 (ARMv8‑A, 64‑bit), running at **1.5–1.8 GHz** depending on the chip revision and
  board cooler. [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raspberry_Pi_4)
- **Process node:** Fabricated in **28 nm** CMOS. [wikidevi.wi-cat](https://wikidevi.wi-cat.ru/Broadcom_BCM2711)
- **Cache:** Each core has 32 KB L1 instruction + 32 KB L1 data cache, plus a shared **1 MB L2** cache for the whole CPU.
  [raspberrypi](https://www.raspberrypi.com/documentation/computers/processors.html)

### Integrated GPU and multimedia

- **GPU:** Broadcom **VideoCore VI** GPU, offering significantly better graphics and video performance than VideoCore IV in
  earlier Pis. [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raspberry_Pi_4)
- **Graphics APIs:** Supports **OpenGL ES 3.1** and **Vulkan 1.0**, enabling modern 3D graphics workloads.
  [wikidevi.wi-cat](https://wikidevi.wi-cat.ru/Broadcom_BCM2711)
- **Video decode:** Capable of **4Kp60 H.265 (HEVC)** decode and 4K H.264 at 60 fps, plus 1080p encode.
  [cnx-software](https://www.cnx-software.com/2019/06/24/raspberry-pi-4-features-broadcom-bcm2711-processor-up-to-4gb-ram/)

### Memory and I/O

- **Memory:** Interfaces with **LPDDR4** or **LPDDR4X** RAM, typically clocked around 2400–3200 MHz, in configurations up to
  8 GB in Raspberry Pi 4‑class boards. [modberry.techbase](https://modberry.techbase.eu/tag/broadcom-bcm2711/)
- **I/O and peripherals:** Includes:
  - Dual HDMI controller (2× 4K60 outputs).
  - Gigabit Ethernet PHY with IEEE 1588 support.
  - PCIe 2.0 interface (used for some add‑on boards).
  - Standard interfaces such as UART, I²C, SPI, PWM, and the 40‑pin GPIO bus.
    [pip.raspberrypi](https://pip.raspberrypi.com/documents/RP-008248-DS-bcm2711-peripherals.pdf)

### Position in Raspberry Pi ecosystem

- The BCM2711 powers the **Raspberry Pi 4 Model B** and later Compute Module 4‑based devices, delivering roughly **50% more
  CPU performance** than the older BCM2837 (Pi 3) and greatly improved GPU and video capabilities.
  [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raspberry_Pi_4)

In short, the BCM2711 is a 28 nm, quad‑core 64‑bit SoC with a modern VideoCore VI GPU, strong video decode, and rich I/O,
making it the workhorse chip behind the Raspberry Pi 4 family.
