A **Raspberry Pi 4 Model B Rev 1.2** is a standard‑revision board of the Raspberry Pi 4, so its specs follow the usual Pi 4
Model B technical sheet, just with that specific board‑version marking.
[raspberrypi](https://www.raspberrypi.com/products/raspberry-pi-4-model-b/specifications/)

### Basic hardware specs

- **SoC (processor):** Broadcom BCM2711, quad‑core Cortex‑A72 (ARM v8) 64‑bit, typically 1.5–1.8 GHz depending on chip
  stepping. [raspberrypi](https://www.raspberrypi.com/products/raspberry-pi-4-model-b/specifications/)
- **RAM:** Available as 1 GB, 2 GB, 4 GB, or 8 GB LPDDR4‑3200, depending on which variant you have (the board itself is
  physically the same; RAM is fixed at purchase).
  [pip.raspberrypi](https://pip.raspberrypi.com/documents/RP-008341-DS-raspberry-pi-4-datasheet.pdf)
- **Networking:** Gigabit Ethernet plus dual‑band Wi‑Fi (2.4 GHz and 5.0 GHz 802.11ac) and Bluetooth 5.0 + BLE.
  [raspberrypi](https://www.raspberrypi.com/products/raspberry-pi-4-model-b/)

### Ports and I/O

- **USB:** 2 × USB 3.0 (blue) and 2 × USB 2.0 ports.
  [raspberrypi](https://www.raspberrypi.com/products/raspberry-pi-4-model-b/specifications/)
- **Video:** 2 × micro‑HDMI ports supporting up to 4Kp60; 2‑lane MIPI DSI display port.
  [pip.raspberrypi](https://pip.raspberrypi.com/documents/RP-008344-DS-raspberry-pi-4-product-brief.pdf)
- **Camera/display:** 2‑lane MIPI CSI camera port plus the standard 40‑pin GPIO header (fully compatible with earlier Pi
  models). [datacapturecontrol](https://datacapturecontrol.com/articles/io-devices/single-board-computers/raspberry-pi-4b)
- **Audio:** 3.5 mm 4‑pole stereo audio/composite jack.
  [raspberrypi](https://www.raspberrypi.com/products/raspberry-pi-4-model-b/specifications/)

### Physical and power

- **Size:** About credit‑card sized, roughly 85 mm × 56 mm.
  [datacapturecontrol](https://datacapturecontrol.com/articles/io-devices/single-board-computers/raspberry-pi-4b)
- **Power:** 5 V via USB‑C; maximum power draw is roughly 3–4 W under typical load, but can be higher with heavy CPU/GPU or
  many peripherals. [pip.raspberrypi](https://pip.raspberrypi.com/documents/RP-008344-DS-raspberry-pi-4-product-brief.pdf)

In short, a Pi 4 Model B Rev 1.2 is a fully capable single‑board computer with modern ARM CPU, up to 8 GB RAM, true gigabit
Ethernet, dual‑4K HDMI, and plenty of USB and GPIO for projects, just like other Pi 4B variants.
